# Video Presentation Scripts & Shot List

This guide provides the exact talking points, on-screen shot lists, and timing cues for recording the mandatory submission video (Maximum 10 minutes total: Part 1 ≤ 5 min, Part 2 ≤ 5 min).

---

## Part 1: Client Pitch (Commercial Presentation)
- **Target Audience:** UK Energy Utility Power Procurement Team / Commercial Head of Trading
- **Target Duration:** 4:30 – 5:00 minutes
- **Tone:** Confident, commercial, outcome-oriented, professional

### Shot List & Script:
| Timing | On-Screen Action / Visual | Spoken Script / Narration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:30** | Opening on Portal Landing Page (`http://localhost:3000`). Point cursor to the branding and status badges. | *"Hello team. Every morning, your power purchase desk faces a high-stakes question: how much electricity must we buy in the day-ahead auction to meet consumer demand, after accounting for national wind generation? If your wind forecast over-predicts by even 2,000 MW, your utility is caught short in real-time, facing volatile Balancing Mechanism cashout charges. Today, I'm excited to present the customized Day-Ahead Wind Decision & Accuracy Portal built specifically for your trading desk."* |
| **0:30 – 2:00** | On **Decision Desk** tab. Change delivery day to `2026-01-15` and cutoff to `09:00`. Hover over the Intraday Chart and switch to the **Forecast Revisions Evolution** subtab. | *"Let's look at the Decision Desk. Here, you see exactly what your trader knew at the moment of bidding. For delivery on January 15, 2026, with your gate closure cutoff at 09:00 AM on D-1, our portal freezes the latest published forecast prior to that second. In this intraday chart across all 48 settlement periods, the cyan line is your frozen decision forecast, the emerald line is the actual outturn, and any shaded red region instantly alerts you to uncontracted shortfall risk. By clicking 'Forecast Revisions Evolution', your analysts can see how meteorological models evolved over successive runs, giving you clear visibility into forecast stability before you submit bids."* |
| **2:00 – 3:30** | Click on **Accuracy & Risk** in top navbar. Point to the KPI cards: MAE (2,145 MW), Net Bias (+1,852 MW), Shortfall Risk Share (84.9%). Scroll to Diurnal profile and Horizon curve. | *"Turning to the Accuracy & Risk suite: across January 2026, the national wind forecast exhibited a Mean Absolute Error of 2,145 MW, or 18.5% normalized error. Crucially, the forecast had a net positive bias of +1,852 MW—meaning wind over-predicted in 85% of all half-hours. For your desk, this proves that unadjusted day-ahead forecasts pose a persistent shortfall risk. Our diurnal and horizon analysis further reveals that as gate closure approaches from 48 hours to same-day, forecast error drops by over 600 MW, providing actionable intelligence on when to hedge in intraday markets."* |
| **3:30 – 4:30** | Focus on the **Recommended Thermal Procurement Buffer** card. Interact with the **Target Confidence slider** (e.g. adjust between 85% and 95%). | *"Here is the most impactful feature for your buying decisions: the Recommended Safety Buffer. Instead of guessing how much extra thermal power or gas capacity to procure, the portal gives you an exact MW recommendation. At a 90% confidence level, it recommends holding an average buffer of approximately 4,275 MW. And this isn't theoretical: our honest rolling walk-forward backtest proves that an ex-ante buffer calibrated strictly on prior days would have successfully protected 89.3% of all delivery periods from uncontracted imbalances."* |
| **4:30 – 5:00** | Switch to **Methodology & Specs** view, showing timeline and formulas. Summary remarks. | *"In summary, this pilot proves that with clean Elexon BMRS data integration and strict anti-leakage decision rules, your desk can eliminate guesswork, protect against peak cashout penalties, and make quantified, confident day-ahead purchase decisions. Thank you, and we look forward to moving into phase two."* |

## Part 2: Technical Lead Review (Engineering & Architecture)
- **Target Audience:** Internal Technical Lead / Engineering Reviewer
- **Target Duration:** 4:30 – 5:00 minutes
- **Tone:** Technical, precise, rigorous, highlighting clean architecture, tests, and anti-leakage guarantees

### Shot List & Script:
| Timing | On-Screen Action / Visual | Spoken Script / Narration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5:00 – 6:00** | Show `docs/architecture.md` diagram and `backend/` directory structure in IDE. | *"Hi everyone. In Part 2, I'll walk through the software architecture, engineering decisions, and quality guarantees. Our stack consists of a FastAPI Python 3.12 backend, an ingested SQLite data store, and a Next.js React 19 frontend with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Recharts. Data flows from Elexon's official BMRS stream API through our chunked ingestion engine, into an indexed local database, through pure analytical calculation modules, and into typed REST endpoints."* |
| **6:00 – 7:30** | Open `backend/analysis/selection.py` and run `pytest backend/tests/test_selection.py -v` in terminal. | *"The intellectual core of this application is the anti-leakage selection rule. Day-ahead trading decisions are strictly made at D-1 before gate closure. In `selection.py`, we localize the cutoff time in Europe/London local time, convert to UTC, and select the latest WINDFOR forecast published at or before that exact timestamp. Forecasts published even one minute after gate closure are physically impossible for the trader to have possessed and are strictly forbidden. In `test_selection.py`, we have unit tests proving that post-cutoff publishes are never chosen, even if they are closer to actuals, and that periods with no qualifying forecast remain cleanly null rather than being silently filled."* |
| **7:30 – 8:30** | Open `backend/analysis/metrics.py` and show `compute_safety_buffer_analysis` and `compute_horizon_analysis`. Run `pytest backend/tests/test_metrics.py -v`. | *"For quantitative accuracy evaluation, all calculations in `metrics.py` are pure, stateless functions. We compute MAE, RMSE, Bias, and nMAE. For the safety buffer backtest, we implemented a rolling walk-forward backtest: for any delivery day D, the buffer is calibrated strictly on historical shortfalls from days 1 to D-1. We test this rigorously in `test_metrics.py` to ensure zero look-ahead bias. In addition, our horizon analysis dynamically measures forecast degradation across 0-6h up to 48-72h lead horizons."* |
| **8:30 – 9:30** | Open `backend/app/routes.py`, `backend/tests/test_api.py`, and `frontend/src/lib/api.ts`. Run full test suites: `pytest backend/tests` and `cd frontend && npm test`. | *"Our API contract is strictly typed using Pydantic v2 schemas. Every endpoint has parameter validation, ensuring dates outside January 2026 or invalid cutoff formats return clean 422 HTTP errors. We have 25 unit and integration tests passing in 1.4 seconds on the backend, and 4 Vitest component tests on the frontend. We also enforce code cleanliness using Ruff and TypeScript strict mode."* |
| **9:30 – 10:00** | Show `docker-compose.yml`, `backend/Dockerfile`, `frontend/Dockerfile`, and `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. | *"Finally, the system is fully containerized. Multi-stage Dockerfiles for both backend and frontend allow the entire stack to be built and run with a single command: `docker compose up --build`. The SQLite database is lightweight at 3.1 MB and completely self-contained. The codebase is fully anonymous, reproducible with `make ingest`, and ready for cloud deployment. Thank you."* |
